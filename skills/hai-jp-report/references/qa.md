# Final source-versus-Japanese review

Compare final JP directly with the authoritative source. Fluent Japanese, clean lint, and successful back-translation are insufficient evidence of fidelity.

## Inputs

Read the complete source for the requested scope, final JP, approved glossary/style, and this checklist. Know which source version governs. For independent review, use a fresh context without the translator's rationale or self-assessment.

If the original language is unavailable, use original JA as the editing baseline and mark original-language translation fidelity **not assessed**. Do not infer a VI/EN source by translating JA backwards.

## Review order

1. **Decision:** Preserve recommendation strength, scores, approval states, headline conclusions, and scope.
2. **Evidence:** Distinguish facts, unverified company claims, hypotheses, estimates, opinions, and overrides with their attribution.
3. **Logic:** Preserve negation, quantifiers, exceptions, necessary/sufficient conditions, AND/OR relationships.
4. **Context:** Preserve actors, customers, geography, samples, periods, and baselines.
5. **Data:** Check each number with unit, precision, sign, currency, denominator, period, and table position. Verify equivalent unit conversions arithmetically; do not repair source errors silently.
6. **Coverage:** Check each section, paragraph, list item, table row, caption, footnote, and source entry. Every source claim survives; every JP claim has support. In requested summaries, retain all decision-relevant conditions and limitations.
7. **Sources/format:** Citations support the same claims; URLs, IDs, anchors, references, and required fields remain. The requested artifact is complete and readable.
8. **Japanese:** Check terminology, register, grammar, collocations, and references. Separate style issues from meaning errors.
9. **Report branding:** No newly added third-party skill/author credits, logos, watermarks, software license text, or implementation notes in the report, footers, comments, slide notes, or export metadata. Keep original evidence citations; software notices belong in the installed package.

Review title and executive summary separately after the body. A caveat deep in the report does not repair an overconfident headline.

## Findings

Return actionable findings rather than a quality score:

| Location | Severity | Source / JP evidence | Correction | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Section or table/cell | Critical / Major / Minor | Short exact excerpts | Minimal suggested JP | What changed or is unclear |

- **Critical:** Could reverse a decision or materially change obligations, economic magnitude, eligibility, or the source of a claim.
- **Major:** Changes meaning, evidence status, actor, condition, coverage, important terminology, or citation support.
- **Minor:** Wording or typography without changed meaning.

These are workflow labels, not certification. Identify source ambiguities separately from translation errors. Do not manufacture findings. Review-only responses may suggest sentence corrections but must not silently replace the report.

## Completion

Fix confirmed critical/major errors and recheck affected headings, summaries, tables, citations, and repeated terms. A known material ambiguity prevents calling its passage final; finish unaffected sections and state the specific open question. Do not claim skipped checks passed.

Unless report-only output was requested, briefly state outside the report: source and scope, actual review method (independent reviewer or self-review; scripts or manual), and unresolved questions or that no meaning-changing discrepancy was found in this review.

Do not claim “100% accurate,” “native reviewed,” “JTF certified,” or superiority over ordinary GPT without corresponding evidence. A model review remains a model review.
