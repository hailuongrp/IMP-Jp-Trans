# EE — NoriFlow

## Recommendation
Defer the investment decision. Consider a four-week pilot only if at least two target companies sign paid pilot agreements AND the budget is no more than JPY 3 million. Meeting these conditions does not guarantee approval. This is a recommendation, not an approved plan. [E1]

## Findings
The company says it has 14 paying client companies; this has not been independently verified. Of six interviewees, four expressed interest. None committed to purchase. This does not establish that there is no market demand.

A scenario estimates annual revenue of JPY 120 million, not profit. The observed error rate fell from 9% to 6%, a decrease of 3 percentage points, across 40 trial records. Every record required manual review without exception. [E2]
